My Textures: a starter kit for a texture mod.

Installed as it is, this kit turns the town grass (spring to autumn) into a
bold two-green check, so you can see a texture mod work before you paint
anything. The pictures are in textures/: 27 copies of one 64x64 picture,
one for each name and palette the game draws the grass with.

The quickest way to start your own is the texture inspector in the game,
which makes a ready-to-paint mod with the textures you pick. This kit is for
when you already know the texture names you want, or want to see one work.

1. Open mod.json in any text editor. Change "yourname" in "id" and "authors"
   to your own name (lower case letters, digits and dashes), and give the mod
   a "name" and "description" of your own.
2. Paint over the pictures in textures/, or delete them and put your own
   .dds or .png files there, each under the exact name the game uses. Find
   names with the texture inspector: open
   https://playaconline.com/play/?moddev=1 and look in Settings, Mods,
   Developer.
3. ZIP it: the folder (Finder Compress, Windows Send to) or the files inside it,
   either works, as long as mod.json is at the top or in the one folder. Install
   it in the game:
   Settings, Mods, Install from a file, Choose ZIP. Then Restart now.

Guide: https://playaconline.com/modding/guides/textures/
