My Lines: a starter kit for writing villager lines.

What it does: once a day, each villager you talk to says one of your lines,
then carries on with their usual hello.

1. Open mod.json in any text editor. Change "yourname" in "id" and "authors"
   to your own name (lower case letters, digits and dashes), and give the mod
   a "name" and "description" of your own.
2. Open messages/en.json. Write your lines as "line1", "line2", "line3" and
   so on, numbered with no gaps. Each line is up to 400 bytes. Keep the
   quotes, and the commas between lines.
3. ZIP it: the folder (Finder Compress, Windows Send to) or the files inside it,
   either works, as long as mod.json is at the top or in the one folder. Install
   it in the game:
   Settings, Mods, Install from a file, Choose ZIP. Then Restart now.
4. Talk to a villager.

Guide: https://playaconline.com/modding/guides/lines/
